Scope: how AO3 preface HTML embedded in an EPUB is parsed into structured

## AO3 Metadata and Tags

`AO3MetadataExtractor` parses AO3 EPUB preface HTML with SwiftSoup and returns `AO3MetadataRecord`. `LibrarySession` tries spine[0] first (AO3 EPUBs reliably place the preface there), falling back through the first 8 spine items for non-standard files or external fanfiction tools, stores successful extraction in `ao3_metadata`, and stores skipped/failed attempts in `ao3_extraction_diagnostics`. Extraction runs concurrently via a capped `TaskGroup` (up to 8 books in flight at once), flushing to the DB every 10 books or every 2 seconds, whichever comes first, with `Task.yield()` between flushes so read queries can cut in.

Extracted fields include story URL, work ID, author username, kudos, word count, chapter counts, completion, language, dates, fandoms, relationships, characters, additional tags, categories, AO3 collections, and AO3 series.

Series metadata is cached in `series_cache`; Calibre series data is inserted as fallback.

### Multi-Engine External Fanfiction Parsers (FanFicFare, FicLab, FicHub)

In addition to official AO3 EPUB downloads (`archiveofourown.org`), `AO3MetadataExtractor` and `LibrarySession` support EPUB frontmatter generated by external tools archiving AO3 works:
- **Fast-path detection**: If the first spine item contains `#preface` and `archiveofourown.org`, the native high-speed DOM parser is used immediately.
- **FanFicFare (`FanFicFareParser`)**: Identifies `class="fanficfare_titlepage"` or `.metadata` blocks generated by FanFicFare. Extracts AO3 work ID, metadata fields (fandoms, characters, relationships, tags, series, kudos, word counts), and summary notes. Non-AO3 stories (e.g. from FanFiction.net, Fimfiction) are intentionally detected and skipped with clear diagnostics ("Non-AO3 story in FanFicFare preface").
- **FicLab (`FicLabParser`)**: Identifies `class="ficlab-title"` or `meta[content*='ficlab']`. Parses FicLab's two-column metadata tables, badge structures, and tag links.
- **FicHub (`FicHubParser`)**: Identifies `class="fichub-title"` or FicHub table headers, parsing standard story links, metadata spans, and AO3 series details.

Spine scanning examines up to the first 8 spine items to locate valid title pages and metadata prefaces across varying tool generator formats. Non-AO3 stories from external aggregators are recorded as non-AO3 skips without cluttering extraction diagnostics as broken works.

### Tag Seeds & Synonym Resolution
Configured AO3 tag seed databases (`ao3_tag_seeds.db`) are queried **directly in read-only mode** via `AO3TagSeedDatabaseConfig.shared`. Tag seed rows are no longer duplicated into each library's `ambrosia_meta.db`, saving ~117 MB per library and eliminating duplicate tables (`canonical_tags`, `tag_synonyms`, `tag_parent_links`, `tag_subtag_sections`). Any legacy copies of these tables in existing `ambrosia_meta.db` files are automatically dropped and vacuumed on library open.

`canonicalTerm(for:)` and `expandedTerms(for:)` on `AmbrosiaMetaDB` delegate directly to `AO3TagSeedDatabaseConfig.shared`, providing canonicalization and synonym expansion across search and filter pipelines without redundant disk copies.

### Extraction Diagnostics & Debugging Failed Works
When extraction encounters a non-AO3 EPUB or malformed preface structure, the failure reason is recorded in `ao3_extraction_diagnostics (calibre_id, story_url, reason, attempted_at)`.

In **Settings ▸ Data**, users can view extraction progress (Extracted vs. Failed vs. Total) and click **"View Failed Works"** to open a diagnostic inspection sheet. The sheet displays the book title, author, and specific parsing error message (e.g. missing preface elements, malformed HTML, or unparseable metadata), with a button to open each failed book directly in the reader for inspection.
